# Test 1 — Inward Journey marketing website

## Objective

Build a beautiful, complete marketing/explainer website for **Inward Journey**,
a contemplative 3D browser game. This is a craftsmanship test: the site must be
genuinely beautiful, not merely functional.

## About the game (facts — use these, do not invent lore)

- Inward Journey is a contemplative 3D world you explore in the browser, rooted
  in the Law of One material (the L/L Research channeling archive).
- The heart of the game: 22 archetypes. You sit with an archetype, ask it
  questions or name a feeling, and it answers in voice.
- The world: a sunrise-lake home base; the Island of Mind (floating forests,
  libraries, dream landscapes, mirrors, fog); the Island of Body (mountains).
- Gentle piano score, minimal UI, ink-drawing art aesthetic.
- Free, runs in the browser. Play it at
  https://vy6ycr7tcc-debug.github.io/Animation/
- Touchstones: Monument Valley, Sky, Myst.

## The site

Single-page site — `index.html` plus styles and script, all under
`site-inward-journey/`:

1. Hero — the game's name, one true sentence about what it is, a Play
   call-to-action linking to the live game.
2. The concept — what a contemplative game is; the Law of One roots. One or two
   honest, plain paragraphs. No hype.
3. The world — the sunrise-lake home, the Island of Mind, the Island of Body.
4. The 22 archetypes — what it means to sit with one and be answered in voice.
5. How to play — browser, free, minimal UI; link to the live game.
6. Footer — quiet credits.

## Design bar (mandatory)

- Read `style/VISUAL_QUALITY.md`, `style/ANIMATION_QUALITY.md`, and
  `style/STYLE_GUIDE.md` first. Follow them on every section. Confirm in your
  summary that you actually followed them.
- The site must feel like the game: ethereal calm, deep blues and golds,
  moonlit, unhurried. Nothing about it should look like a generic
  startup landing page.
- Real copy throughout — write it yourself from the facts above. No lorem
  ipsum, no placeholder text, no "coming soon."
- Typography carries the design: choose a serif/display pairing with intent.

## Technical constraints

- Pure static HTML/CSS/JS. No npm, no build step, no framework that needs
  compiling. CDN libraries are fine.
- All paths relative. The site must work from any static host.
- Responsive: must look right on a phone.

## Verification (do not skip)

- Screenshot every section yourself in a headless browser and iterate until it
  is genuinely beautiful. "It builds" is not done. If a section looks cheap,
  redo it.
- Commit and push to your branch after each milestone.

## Scope

This session builds ONLY the website in `site-inward-journey/`. Do not touch
anything else in the repo. Do not ask clarifying questions — decide and note
it.
